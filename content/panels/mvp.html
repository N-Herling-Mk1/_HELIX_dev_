MVP — Week 10 Definition

HELIX is research, but a week-10 MVP still pays off: it forces the summer to end with something demonstrable rather than open-ended. The bar is proof of concept — each of the three ideas shown to work at all, end-to-end on real ATLAS data — plus the unifying narrative made legible in one place. Completeness (tuned models, note-grade validation) is Fall's job, not the MVP's.

Definition of done — by Fri 8/7

The MVP ships when all three gates are cleared (even shallowly) and consolidated into the HELIX hub:

  • P1 — Language. The bigram runs on real mk3 CSVs and produces an AUC plus the Ψsig/Ψbkg heatmap; the Evt2Vec Phase-1 InfoNCE encoder trains without collapse, with a linear-probe AUC that beats raw features.
  • P2 — MaxEnt background. The surprise score S(x) is demonstrated as a complementary, NN-free handle in ≥1 region (endcap banked); the barrel failure mode is fixed-or-characterized via the Round-3 Poisson run.
  • P3 — Bayesian bounds. BLL-HMC converges on the four models (R̂ ≈ 1, healthy ESS), yields σoverall and σboundary, and the posterior is rendered against the MaxEnt floor H(pk).
  • Hub & narrative. The three results are consolidated into the HELIX documentation hub, each tied to its kill condition, with the Jaynes → InfoNCE-is-Z → one-spine story legible end-to-end.

Minimum vs. target

Minimum is the gate language above — pass/fail on real data. Target adds: both tokenizations (S1 + S3) compared, the WEAT/WEFAT bias probes run, and the Laplace cross-check agreeing with HMC. The signal-MC validation is target-but-MC-gated (Branch A); under Branch B it moves to Fall without blocking the MVP.

Out of scope — defers to Fall

  • CutFormer (Phase 2a) and Bayesian CutFormer (Phase 2b, 1000+ HMC samples)
  • MaxEnt validation to analysis-note grade — stress tests, systematic bootstrap, formal orthogonality (Levels 3–4)
  • Full RRM-4 across all four models; full cutflow ΔIk audit
  • Tuned / production embeddings
