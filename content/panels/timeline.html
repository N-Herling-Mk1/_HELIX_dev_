Project Timeline

HELIX runs a two-phase arc on its own track. Summer 2026 (10 weeks) proves that each of three ideas works at all, end-to-end on real ATLAS data — the bar is proof of concept, not completeness. Fall 2026 (15 weeks) is depth: validation to note/thesis grade, CutFormer, full Run 3 integration. The summer is built to demonstrate one claim end-to-end, even shallowly: Jaynes → InfoNCE-is-Z → one shared partition-function spine that yields (a) a learned event embedding, (b) a complementary background handle, and (c) a physics-derived epistemic floor. Three PoCs, one spine.

FORGE runs in parallel as the Bayesian-methods sandbox and does not compete for these ten weeks. Its validated BLL-HMC technique converges into HELIX around week 5–6 — deliberately just ahead of the P3 run, so P3 applies a method that has already been shaken out rather than debugging convergence from scratch.

Signal-MC branch

Several P2 steps and the P3 remedy story need the signal mass points (mS5/16/35/55) runnable on atlng01 this summer. The sprint is built so that either branch clears all three gates — MC status shifts only when the MC-specific validation lands, not whether the sprint succeeds.

Branch A — MC available

Wk 3–4 includes the signal-MC surprise validation (signal sits in the high-S tail); P3 can frame the “diverse MC fixes underdetermined weights” remedy directly.

Branch B — MC unavailable

P2 PoC is met via data-only complementarity + barrel characterization; the signal-MC pass moves to Fall. P3 is unaffected — it runs on the already-trained models; only the MC remedy interpretation defers.
